# Test with Our Sandbox Processors

You **don't** need your own Stripe, Paddle, or any other payment account to build and test a paywall. We provide **two ready-made sandbox payment processors** that any account can connect to any paywall — no credentials, no setup, no waiting.

<Callout type="info">
  Use them to validate your entire integration first. Opening your own payment
  provider account is only necessary when you're ready to charge real customers.
</Callout>

## What you can test with them

Sandbox processors simulate payments end to end, so everything downstream of the checkout behaves the way it will in production:

- **The full checkout flow** — the paywall opens, the customer picks a plan and completes payment
- **Subscriptions** — creation, renewals and trial periods
- **Charges and balances** — token and credit deduction if you use tokenization
- **Access granting** — entitlement checks and feature gating in your app
- **Webhooks** — your endpoint receives events just as it does in live mode
- **Customer portal** — cancellations and plan changes

No real money moves and no real card is ever charged.

## Connecting a sandbox processor

<Steps>

### Step 1: Open the Payment Processor Manager

Go to **Paywalls**, open your paywall, and click **Payment Processor Manager**.

### Step 2: Switch to the Test tab

The drawer has **Live** and **Test** tabs. Both sandbox processors always appear in the **Test** tab, listed alongside any test processors of your own. You'll recognise them by the caption:

> Our sandbox processor — payments are simulated, use test card 4242 4242 4242 4242

### Step 3: Select and save

Tick one or both, then click **Save**. If you connect more than one, set the traffic share for each — or use **Distribute equally**.

### Step 4: Pay with a test card

With the paywall in test mode, open it and complete a purchase using card **4242 4242 4242 4242**, any future expiry date, and any CVC.

</Steps>

## Moving to live

Sandbox processors are **test mode only**. They never appear in the Live tab and cannot process real payments.

When you're ready to accept real money, [create your own payment processor](/docs-v2/payment-processor/create-payment-processor) in live mode and [connect it to the paywall](/docs-v2/payment-processor/connect-payment-processor).

<Callout type="warning">
  A paywall in live mode uses **only** the processors connected in the Live tab.
  If a paywall has nothing but sandbox processors connected, it will have no way
  to take payment once you switch it to live.
</Callout>

## FAQ

<Accordion>
  <Accordion.Item title="Do I need my own Stripe account just to try the platform?">
    No. Our two sandbox processors are available to every account and cover the whole test flow — checkout, subscriptions, webhooks and access granting. You only need your own processor account to accept real payments.
  </Accordion.Item>

  <Accordion.Item title="Can I use a sandbox processor in live mode?">
    No. They're restricted to test mode and won't appear in the Live tab.
  </Accordion.Item>

  <Accordion.Item title="Can I connect both sandbox processors at once?">
    Yes. Connect both and split the traffic between them — this is a good way to rehearse a multi-processor setup before you run one for real.
  </Accordion.Item>

  <Accordion.Item title="Some processors in the list are greyed out. Why?">
    Rows captioned "Managed processor" are platform-owned and can't be selected or edited by yourself. Contact support if you believe you should have access to one.
  </Accordion.Item>
</Accordion>
